Frequently asked questions

Why are some battery readings unavailable?

Android devices expose different battery fields. Current, temperature, charge counter, cycle count and capacity information can be missing or vary by manufacturer. CycleSense leaves unsupported readings unavailable instead of treating them as verified values.

How does App usage stats work?

It is optional and off by default. Turn it on from Settings → Advanced in the app, then grant Usage Access in Android Settings. The app uses the access on-device to show app-usage context. You can revoke it from Android’s Usage Access settings.

How can I remove my data?

CycleSense currently does not have a delete-all button. On Android, open Settings → Apps → CycleSense → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall the app. This does not remove any CSV you already exported or shared. If Android backup is enabled, eligible data may later be restored by the system.

Can I move my session history?

Use Export session history in the app’s Settings to create a CSV and choose a destination in the Android share sheet. The destination’s own privacy and retention practices apply to the copy you share.
